Open **SCENE** (`B`) to choose what stands behind the avatar.

## Background

![The SCENE window with the Cosy Room environment](/screenshots/en/scene.webp)

The **BACKGROUND** section offers:

- **COLOUR**: a solid colour, with chroma-key presets (green, blue, magenta...) or any colour you pick;
- **CLEAR**: a transparent window;
- **IMAGE**: a picture of your choice (**PICK IMAGE**);
- **3D**: a lit 3D environment (`E` toggles it).

<Callout title="Cameras have no alpha">
The virtual camera can't carry transparency. With CLEAR, it sends the avatar over the chosen colour (green by default), so a chroma key in the receiving app still works.
</Callout>

## 3D environments

![The six built-in environments](/screenshots/environments.jpg)

Press `E` (or switch the background to 3D) to stand the avatar in a lit scene. The six built-in scenes are generated by the app at runtime:

| Scene | What it is |
|---|---|
| STUDIO CYCLORAMA | Seamless photo-studio cove, soft key, fill and rim light, two softboxes framing the shot |
| COSY ROOM | Wallpapered corner with a bookshelf, a window at dusk, a rug and a floor lamp that lights the avatar |
| MOOR AT DUSK | Rolling heather, a ring of standing stones, low sun, fog and drifting mist |
| NIGHT CLEARING | Ring of conifers under a starry sky, moonlight and fireflies |
| NEON GRID | Scrolling synthwave grid, striped sun, glowing ridges |
| CONCRETE HALL | Board-formed concrete, columns, light slots, a slot window and an orange rule |
| CUSTOM | Your own `.glb` / `.gltf` scene and an equirectangular `.hdr` / `.exr` sky |

### Your own scene

Load a `.glb` or `.gltf` with **SCENE**, and a sky with **SKY**, or drop the files on the window. A `.gltf` may reference `.bin` and texture files next to it. **NO SKY** removes a custom sky.

- **Placement:** SCALE (0.01–100×), POS X / Y / Z and ROTATE. A loaded scene is centred under the avatar with its lowest point on the floor.
- **FLOOR M** (every scene) moves the scene up or down to meet the avatar's feet.
- A custom HDR sky also lights the avatar and the scene.

The app reopens the last custom scene and sky. **RESET THIS SCENE** restores a scene's look and placement.

### Look

- **Light:** SCENE uses the scene's own lighting; KEY, RIM and SOFT restyle it. **INTENSITY** and **TEMP K** (white balance; 6500 is neutral) apply on top. The lighting is kept within a range where the face neither clips to white nor goes black.
- **Atmosphere:** **FOG** (0.5 is the scene's default) and **GROUND SHADOW** under the avatar.
- **Camera:** **IDLE SWAY** adds a slow drift of about ±1 cm. **DEPTH OF FIELD** (off by default, HIGH quality only) keeps the avatar sharp and blurs the background.
- **Quality:** **LOW** uses a soft blob shadow and no post-processing. **HIGH** adds a real directional shadow and allows depth of field.

Look settings are saved per scene.

<Callout type="warn" title="Performance">
Depth of field noticeably reduces how fast tracking can run, which is why it's off by default. If tracking feels slow in a 3D scene, try LOW quality first.
</Callout>

## Framing

![The same avatar framed BUST, UPPER and FULL](/screenshots/framing.webp)

**02 FRAMING** on the stage picks **BUST**, **UPPER** or **FULL**. Framing scales with the avatar, so very small or very large models are framed sensibly too.

## Capture view

Press `H` (or **CAPTURE VIEW** in the top bar) to hide all the interface and leave only the avatar, for clean window capture in OBS. `Esc` leaves capture view.

The virtual camera always sends this capture view, whether or not it's showing on screen.
