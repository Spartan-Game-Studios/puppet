## The demo

### What happens after 100 hours?

Tracking on the stage, the virtual camera and VMC output lock. The model library still opens and keeps every model you imported.

### Do I lose my models if I buy the full app?

No. The full app picks up the same library, so nothing has to be imported again.

### Is the demo missing features?

No. Until the 100 hours are used up it's the full app.

## Camera

### The camera doesn't start [#camera-doesnt-start]

- **Allow camera access.** The first time you start the camera, your operating system asks for permission. If you said no:
  - **macOS:** System Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera, and switch VPuppet on. Then restart VPuppet.
  - **Windows:** Settings → Privacy & security → Camera, and allow desktop apps to use the camera.
- **Another app may be using the camera.** Close the video-call app or browser tab that holds it, then press **START CAMERA** in the **SOURCE** section.
- **Pick the right camera** in SOURCE if you have more than one.

### Tracking is slow or laggy

- Use a lower tracking mode: FACE (`1`) costs the least, +HANDS (`3`) the most.
- Set **POSE MODEL** to LITE.
- In a 3D environment, choose LOW quality and switch off depth of field.
- The debug overlay (`D`) shows render and tracking frame rates and latency.
- Raise **SMOOTHING** for steadier movement, lower it for a faster response.

### The avatar's face looks off at rest

Sit as you usually do, eyes open, mouth closed, and press **CALIBRATE** (`C`).

## Virtual camera

### VPuppet Virtual Camera isn't listed [#vcam-not-listed]

- **Is the output on?** The camera appears only while **CAMERA OUTPUT** is on (`W`) and VPuppet is running. This is on purpose: no frozen camera is left behind when VPuppet isn't sending.
- **Restart the other app.** Many apps read the camera list only at start.
- **macOS:**
  - VPuppet must be in **/Applications**.
  - The EXTENSION line must read **ACTIVE**. If it says it's waiting for approval, click **OPEN SETTINGS** and approve it.
  - If it says a restart is needed, restart your Mac.
  - Builds you compile yourself are unsigned and can't install the camera; use the [OBS route](./virtual-camera.mdx#obs-route).
- **Windows:** click **REGISTER CAMERA** and accept the administrator prompt. 32-bit apps can't see the camera.
- **Linux:** create the loopback device with the `modprobe` command from the panel; it doesn't survive a reboot unless you configure your system to load it. The device is called **VPuppet**.

### The camera shows WAITING FOR FRAMES

The camera exists but no frames are arriving. Check that VPuppet is running with CAMERA OUTPUT on.

### The background isn't transparent in my video app

Cameras have no transparency. With CLEAR, VPuppet sends the chosen colour (green by default); key it out in the receiving app.

## VMC

### My receiver doesn't move

- Both apps must use the same port (default `39539`) and VPuppet's HOST must point at the receiver (`127.0.0.1` on the same computer, the other PC's local IP otherwise).
- **SEND VMC** must be ticked (`V`), and receiving must be enabled in the other app.
- A firewall can block UDP between two computers.

### The face is frozen in the receiver

If your model is perfect sync and the receiver reads only VRM presets, keep **ALSO SEND VRM PRESETS** on. In VSeeFace, enable perfect sync on the receiving model to use the ARKit blendshapes.

## Models

### Which models work?

Any `.vrm` file, VRM 0.x or 1.0. VPuppet ships no avatar. Check each model's licence badge for what its author allows.

### Can I use FBX or glTF avatars?

Avatars must be VRM. `.fbx` and `.glb` files are used for animation clips and 3D scenes. To turn another model into a VRM, see the [guides](./guides/vroid.mdx).

### I used "Puppet" before

VPuppet was renamed from Puppet. The first launch moves the old settings and library to the new name automatically.
