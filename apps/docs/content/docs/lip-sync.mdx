---
title: Lip sync
description: Drive the mouth from your microphone when the camera can't see it, or together with tracking.
---

Open **LIP SYNC** (`S`). Lip sync is off by default; VPuppet asks for microphone access only when you choose a mode.

## Modes

![The LIP SYNC window: mode, microphone, gate, gain and viseme levels](/screenshots/en/lipsync.webp)

| Mode | What drives the mouth |
|---|---|
| **OFF** | Face tracking only. |
| **FALLBACK** | Your voice, only while your face isn't tracked (eased in and out). |
| **BLEND** | The larger of tracking and voice, shape by shape. |
| **AUDIO** | Your voice only. |

## Settings

- Pick the **microphone**.
- **GATE DB** sets the level below which sound is ignored. Raise it if background noise moves the mouth.
- The meter shows the input level, the gate and each mouth shape as it's detected.

VPuppet matches your voice against five vowels. Preset models get `aa` `ih` `ou` `ee` `oh`; perfect-sync models get `jawOpen`, `mouthFunnel`, `mouthPucker` and `mouthStretch`. The mouth opens quickly (30 ms) and closes a little more slowly (90 ms).

<Callout title="FALLBACK and weak tracking">
FALLBACK switches over only when the face is lost. It can't tell when mouth tracking is merely weak; use BLEND for that.
</Callout>
